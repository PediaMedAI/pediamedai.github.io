Why we're building this

Most AI is built for adults. Pediatric data is scarce—and trust is critical.

Early detection in autism and developmental conditions can change outcomes. Clinicians need interpretable tools, not black boxes. We build AI that fits pediatric practice and scales with our hospital and university partners.
